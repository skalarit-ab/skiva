Skiva privacy policy

Last updated 8 October 2026

Skiva is a music player made by Skalarit AB, Vegagatan 22B, 413 09 Göteborg, Sweden.

What Skiva collects

Skiva collects no personal data and sends nothing over the internet. It has no accounts, ads or analytics, and it shares nothing with anyone.

What Skiva keeps on your device

Skiva reads the music files on your device to play them. It keeps its library, playlists, settings and loudness measurements in its own storage on your device. That data stays on the device, and uninstalling Skiva deletes it.

Permissions

Skiva asks for permission to read music and audio files, so it can find and play your music. It shows the track playing in the system's media controls, so you can control playback from there, and keeps playing while the screen is off or another app is open.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date.
